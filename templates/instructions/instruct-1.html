Instructions

In this experiment your goal is to learn sequences of directions (↑ ↓ ← →). You will see a row of bozes, and your task is to fill in the correct sequence.


Try filling in the boxes below using the arrow keys on your keyboard. You can enter any sequence of directions you like. You can use the Backspace key to delete your most recent answer.

Once you are happy with your answer for a sequene, you can submit it by clicking the "Submit" button below or pressing the Enter key on your keyboard.



After submitting an answer, you may receive feedback about some of the boxes.

Green means that your choice for that box was correct. Red means that your choice for that box was incorrect.

In this example, the second box is red, so you know that your choice for the second box was incorrect. The fourth box is green, so you know that your choice for the fourth box was correct.

Notice that the other boxes did not change color. This means that you did not receive feedback about those answers. You will not always receive feedback for every box, and might sometimes receive feedback for none of the boxes.
