Instructions

During your attempt of a sequence, you may also see parts of the answer left by a previous player who attempted the same sequence.

Parts of the previous player's answer that are hidden from you will appear as "?". How much of their answer is visible will vary from attempt to attempt. Sometimes you may see none of their answers, while other times you may see most of them.

For example, the image below shows what you might see during the task. In this case, your task is to fill in the answers for your sequence, while some of the answers from a previous player on the same sequence are visible to you.




The previous player's answer is not guaranteed to be correct, though you may use it if you find it helpful.
