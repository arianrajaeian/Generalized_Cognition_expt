Instructions


Here is an example of what your page may look like during the experiment:




As you can see, there will always be two sequences on the page at a time. You will have multiple attempts to answer them. During each attempt, you will be randomly assigned to answer one of the two sequences. The sequence you are assigned to will be highlighted (Sequence B in the above example).

Each sequence has a separate correct answer. However, the two sequences may share the same answer for some boxes. These boxes will be highlighted in blue.

If you discover the correct answer for a blue box in one sequence, you have also discovered the correct answer for that box in the other sequence.

White boxes are independent and may have different answers.
